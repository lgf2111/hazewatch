About the data

What you are looking at. NASA publishes the fires it sees from space, free, every day. Two cameras, five satellites. We put both onto one grid: a square counts as burning on a day if either camera saw fire in it.

What is missing. We loaded one week, not the archive, so nothing here says anything about a season or a year. One of NASA's four files came back empty the day we fetched it — VIIRS S-NPP, 0 rows — and it is shown as empty, not filled in.

What this is not. Not a haze forecast, not PSI. The wind is the surface wind at one point per province, and it does not carry the smoke on its own.

Every number is in data/facts.json; the raw files (data/raw) and the code (tools/build_snapshot.py) are on GitHub.
