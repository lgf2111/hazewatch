    Wind windows

    Heuristic, not a forecast. Index = burning cells × wind alignment × (0.5 + 0.5 × dry spell ÷ 5). A day is “aligned” when at least one cell is burning and the wind blows within 60° of the line to Singapore. The thresholds are chosen, not fitted, and nothing here has been checked against air-quality readings. Surface wind at one point per province is not the wind that carries smoke.
